02 / MODEL OVERVIEW

How the model reaches equilibrium

01
⌘

Choose a price

Each provider selects a candidate price while treating the other providers' current prices as fixed.

02
◉

Allocate demand

Customers are distributed using a multinomial-logit share based on price sensitivity.

sᵢ = e−αpᵢ / Σⱼ e−αpⱼ
03
↗

Calculate profit

A provider earns its per-unit margin multiplied by the demand it receives.

πᵢ = (pᵢ − cᵢ) × Dᵢ
04
◎

Check equilibrium

Best responses repeat until prices stop changing materially. This is a grid-based numerical approximation.

!
Know what this model is, and what it is not.

AWS-like, Azure-like and GCP-like are illustrative provider labels. The model has no cloud API connection, and it assigns all modeled demand among the active providers. Real markets also involve service quality, switching costs, capacity, contracts, and customer segments that are not represented here.
